Explain the process of cellular respiration and its significance in ATP production.

verified Model Answer

Cellular respiration is a multi-step metabolic pathway that breaks down glucose to produce Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP), the primary energy currency of the cell.

  • Glycolysis: Occurs in the cytoplasm; anaerobic process that splits glucose into two pyruvate molecules.
  • Krebs Cycle: Occurs in the mitochondrial matrix; generates high-energy electron carriers (NADH and FADH2).
  • Electron Transport Chain: Final stage in the inner mitochondrial membrane where the majority of ATP (32-34 units) is produced.
Key Tip: Ensure you mention the specific locations of each stage within the mitochondria to secure full marks.
